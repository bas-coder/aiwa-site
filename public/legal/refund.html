Refund and Cancellation Policy

Your options and rights when cancelling or requesting a refund on AIWA.

Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains your rights and options when cancelling your AIWA subscription, requesting a refund, or managing your account billing. We want to be fair and transparent about what is and is not refundable.

All payments on the AIWA platform are processed securely by Stripe.

If you have a question about a charge or would like to request a refund, contact us at billing@aiwa.codes with your account email and the details of your request.

1. SaaS Subscription Refunds

1.1 Eligibility for a Refund

We offer a 7-day refund on SaaS subscription payments under the following conditions:

  • Your refund request is submitted within 7 days of the original payment date
  • You have not used any credits during the billing period in question
  • This is your first refund request for a subscription payment

If both conditions are met, you are entitled to a full refund of that billing period's subscription fee.

1.2 Partial Use

If you have used any credits during a billing period, that billing period is non-refundable regardless of how many credits were consumed. We do not offer pro-rated refunds for partial credit usage on subscription plans.

1.3 Subsequent Billing Periods

The 7-day refund window applies to each individual billing period independently. A refund on your first month does not automatically entitle you to a refund on future months.

1.4 How to Request

Email billing@aiwa.codes with:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • The date of the charge you are requesting a refund for
  • A brief reason for your request

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed through Stripe and typically appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

2. AppSumo Lifetime Deal Refunds

2.1 Refund Window

AppSumo lifetime deal purchases are eligible for a refund within 60 days of the original purchase date, in line with AppSumo's standard buyer protection period.

2.2 Full Refund

You are entitled to a full refund if:

  • Your request is made within the 60-day window
  • You have not used any credits since activating your AppSumo deal

2.3 Pro-Rated Refund

If you have used credits during the 60-day window, a pro-rated refund will be calculated as follows:

Refund amount = Purchase price minus the value of credits consumed

The value of consumed credits is calculated at the standard credit rate applicable to your lifetime deal tier at the time of purchase.

Example: If you purchased a lifetime deal for $97 and consumed credits equivalent to $30 in value, your refund would be $67.

2.4 How to Request

AppSumo lifetime deal refunds should be initiated through AppSumo's standard refund process via your AppSumo account. If you have difficulty with the AppSumo process, you may also contact us directly at billing@aiwa.codes and we will assist in coordinating the refund.

3. Credit Top-Up Refunds

3.1 Unused Credits

If you have purchased a credit top-up bundle and have not used any of the credits, you may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase.

3.2 Partially Used Credits

Credit bundles where any credits have been consumed are non-refundable. We do not offer pro-rated refunds on credit top-up bundles.

3.3 How to Request

Email billing@aiwa.codes with your account email and the date of the credit purchase.

4. Cancellation

4.1 How to Cancel

You can cancel your AIWA subscription at any time through your account settings under Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

4.2 What Happens After Cancellation

When your subscription is cancelled:

  • You retain access to your paid plan features until the end of the current billing period
  • At the end of the billing period, your account moves to the free tier
  • Your projects and data are retained and accessible on the free tier
  • Unused credits from your paid plan allocation that were included in your subscription do not carry over to the free tier after cancellation
  • Any purchased credit top-ups that remain unused are retained in your account and remain accessible on the free tier

4.3 Reactivation

You can reactivate a cancelled subscription at any time. Reactivating restores access to all paid plan features from the date of reactivation. A new billing cycle begins on the reactivation date.

5. Exceptions and Special Circumstances

5.1 Technical Issues

If you experienced a significant technical issue with our Platform that prevented you from using the Service and we were unable to resolve it within a reasonable timeframe, you may be eligible for a refund or credit at our discretion, even outside the standard refund window. Contact billing@aiwa.codes with details of the issue.

5.2 Duplicate Charges

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same billing period, contact us immediately at billing@aiwa.codes. We will investigate and issue a refund promptly for any confirmed duplicate charges.

5.3 Unauthorised Charges

If you believe a charge was made to your payment method without your authorisation, contact us at billing@aiwa.codes immediately. We will investigate and work with you and Stripe to resolve the situation.

5.4 Abuse of Refund Policy

We reserve the right to decline refund requests where we have reasonable evidence that the refund policy is being abused, including repeated cycles of purchasing and refunding or requesting refunds following a violation of our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy.

6. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us at billing@aiwa.codes before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Most billing issues can be resolved quickly and directly. Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in the suspension of your account pending investigation.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund and Cancellation Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email and posted on our Platform with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For refund requests, billing questions, or cancellation assistance:

Email: billing@aiwa.codes Platform: aiwa.codes

We aim to respond to all billing and refund inquiries within 3 business days.

This Refund and Cancellation Policy is provided for informational purposes. AIWA recommends consulting a qualified legal professional for advice specific to your jurisdiction and circumstances.
