Cookie Policy

How AIWA uses cookies and similar technologies, and how to control them.

Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how AIWA ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our platform at aiwa.codes ("Platform"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control how they are used.

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to the website owner.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period of time or until you delete them
  • First-party cookies: Cookies set by AIWA directly
  • Third-party cookies: Cookies set by our partners and service providers

2. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use similar tracking technologies including:

  • Local storage: A browser feature that allows websites to store data on your device that persists beyond a browser session
  • Session storage: Similar to local storage but cleared when your browser session ends
  • Pixel tags and web beacons: Small invisible image files embedded in pages or emails that allow us to track certain actions
  • Analytics scripts: Code snippets from analytics providers that run in your browser to collect usage data

For simplicity, we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies" throughout this policy.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Platform to function. Without them, core features like account login, session management, and security cannot work. These cookies cannot be disabled.

PurposeDescription
AuthenticationKeeps you logged in during your session
SecurityProtects against CSRF attacks and other security threats
Session managementMaintains your session state across pages
Load balancingDistributes traffic across our servers for reliability

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we use on our Platform.

PurposeDescription
User preferencesRemembers your settings such as theme, language, and display preferences
Editor stateSaves your position and state within the AIWA editor between visits
Onboarding progressTracks your progress through onboarding flows

3.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Platform. The data collected is aggregated and used to improve the Platform, not to identify individual users.

ProviderPurpose
Analytics toolsTracks page views, feature usage, session duration, and user flows
Error trackingCaptures technical errors and helps us debug issues
Performance monitoringMeasures load times and platform performance

3.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be used to track visitors across websites and show you relevant advertisements. They are set by our advertising partners.

PurposeDescription
RetargetingAllows us to show AIWA advertisements to users who have visited our Platform
Campaign trackingMeasures the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns
Conversion trackingTracks whether users who clicked on an advertisement went on to sign up or make a purchase

You can opt out of marketing cookies through our cookie preference centre or through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Platform are set by third-party services we use. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. Key third parties include:

  • Stripe: Payment processing. Stripe sets cookies necessary to process payments securely. See Stripe's cookie policy at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Analytics providers: For usage analysis and platform improvement.
  • AI model providers: Processing your prompts to generate applications. These providers do not set cookies on your browser directly but do process data as described in our Privacy Policy.

We do not control third-party cookies and are not responsible for the practices of third-party providers.

6. Your Cookie Choices

6.1 Cookie Preference Centre

When you first visit our Platform, you will be shown a cookie consent banner. You can choose to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customise your preferences. You can update your preferences at any time through the cookie settings link in the footer of our Platform.

6.2 Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Set up notifications when cookies are received

For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, visit your browser's help pages:

  • Chrome: chrome://settings/cookies
  • Firefox: about:preferences#privacy
  • Safari: Preferences, Privacy tab
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions

Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will affect the functionality of our Platform. You may not be able to log in or use core features if these cookies are disabled.

6.3 Opt-Out of Analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

6.4 Opt-Out of Advertising Cookies

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many advertisers through the Digital Advertising Alliance at optout.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative at optout.networkadvertising.org.

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that signals to websites that you do not want your online activity tracked. There is currently no industry standard for how websites should respond to Do Not Track signals. We will update this policy if a standard is established.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for legal and regulatory reasons. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
