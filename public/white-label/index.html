Resale

You set the price. Your customers pay you.

Buy credits from AIWA at a flat $0.70 each. Package them into your customer offers and set your resale prices within the platform's supported pricing limits. Customer payments go through your connected Stripe account.

A simple resale example

ItemIllustrative amount
Credits included in your customer offer200
Your credit cost: 200 × $0.70$140
Price you charge the customer$200
Difference before other expenses$60

Illustrative pricing, not a preset plan or earnings guarantee. The $60 difference is before your program fee, payment fees, refunds, taxes, support, and other expenses. Actual results depend on your offer and costs.

Your credit pool funds customer allocations. Keep it funded so purchases and renewals can receive their credits. Any supported free credit allowance is funded by you too.
