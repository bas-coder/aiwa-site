## 1. Creative thesis

This is **not** an AIWA reseller pitch.

Primary job: make a digital / GHL / web / marketing agency (or software service seller) believe they can **own a branded AI app-building platform** and monetize it — brand, domain, Stripe, pricing, customers — with AIWA as the infrastructure underneath.

Tone: plainspoken, concrete, CEO-clear. Short sentences. Show the money flow. No hype EDM. No income promises.

**ICP (from offer truth):** Digital Agencies · GHL Agencies · Web Design Agencies · Marketing Agencies · SaaS Entrepreneurs · Technology Consultants · Software Service Providers.

## 10. Legal / truth constraints

- **No income promises.** Never “you will make $X/month.”  
- Illustrative math only; label on-screen.  
- Partner credit rate **$0.10** vs standard **$0.20** — say rates **may change**.  
- **1,000 credits are one-time**, not monthly.  
- DFY marketing website: **first 25** qualifying partners only.  
- Not a reseller account — full platform license / branded experience.  
- AIWA powers technology; customer-facing brand is the partner’s.  
- Do not invent features not in offer-config / live product.
